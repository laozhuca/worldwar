WORLDWAR Trench Command

The Western Front, 1916. Between our lines and theirs lies no man's land: mud, craters and wire. Machine guns cover every metre of it. Take the enemy headquarters in the far east, or break their army.

The war

  • Supply convoys arrive every 20s with men, ammunition and supplies. Captured enemy trenches increase the flow.
  • Machine guns massacre troops in the open. Walking troops under fire get pinned and crawl.
  • Barbed wire slows everyone to a crawl. Shellfire cuts it.
  • Artillery kills, cuts wire, damages trenches and suppresses defenders, who can barely shoot back. Shells don't care whose side you're on.
  • Trenches cut casualties sharply. Reinforced trenches (level 2–3) cut them further.

How to win ground

  1. Bombard the enemy wire to cut lanes through it.
  2. Bombard the trench you want so the gunners keep their heads down.
  3. Charge just as the barrage lifts. Charging troops can't be pinned.
  4. Hold the trench, reinforce the squads, then dig in and wire the new line.
